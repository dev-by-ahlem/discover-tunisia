Where to Go

Tunisia's Most Unforgettable Places

Tunisia is packed with hidden gems waiting to be discovered. Whether you're drawn to ancient history, desert adventures, coastal villages, or vibrant city life — there is a destination here for every type of traveller.

Must See

Sidi Bou Said

A magical cliffside village of blue doors and white walls overlooking the Mediterranean. One of Tunisia's most iconic and photogenic spots — perfect for photography lovers and romantic getaways.

  • Location: Tunis Area
  • Best Time: April – October
  • Known For: Blue & white architecture, sea views
UNESCO Site

Carthage

Walk through the ruins of one of the ancient world's greatest civilisations. Carthage is a UNESCO World Heritage Site sitting on a hill overlooking the Gulf of Tunis — a must for every history lover.

  • Location: Tunis Area
  • Best Time: March – May, Sept – Nov
  • Known For: Roman ruins, ancient history
UNESCO Site

El Jem Amphitheatre

One of the best-preserved Roman amphitheatres in the world. This jaw-dropping colosseum in the heart of Tunisia seats 35,000 people and rivals the Colosseum in Rome.

  • Location: Central Tunisia
  • Best Time: March – June
  • Known For: Roman architecture, history
Adventure

Douz & The Sahara

The gateway to the Sahara Desert. Ride camels at sunset, sleep under a billion stars, and experience the silence and magic of the world's greatest desert. An adventure you will never forget.

  • Location: South Tunisia
  • Best Time: October – March
  • Known For: Camel rides, dunes, stargazing
Spiritual

Kairouan Mosque

One of the oldest and most important mosques in the Islamic world. The Great Mosque of Kairouan dates back to 670 AD and is a stunning example of early Islamic architecture.

  • Location: Central Tunisia
  • Best Time: All year round
  • Known For: Islamic heritage, architecture
Port City

Bizerte Port

A charming port city with colourful boats, a beautiful old medina, and a relaxed atmosphere. Tunisia's most northerly city is an underrated gem far from the tourist crowds.

  • Location: North Tunisia
  • Best Time: May – September
  • Known For: Old port, medina, beaches
Island Life

Djerba

Tunisia's largest island, famous for its white villages, turquoise sea, and rich Jewish and Berber heritage. A paradise for beach lovers and culture seekers with a relaxed island atmosphere.

  • Location: South Coast
  • Best Time: April – October
  • Known For: Beaches, heritage, island life
Nature

Korbus

A hidden coastal village famous for its natural hot springs that flow directly into the sea. A truly unique and deeply relaxing experience surrounded by dramatic coastal scenery on Cap Bon.

  • Location: Cap Bon
  • Best Time: October – April
  • Known For: Natural hot springs, sea views
UNESCO Site

Sousse

A vibrant coastal city with a UNESCO-listed medina, beautiful beaches, and a lively atmosphere. Known as the "Pearl of the Sahel", Sousse perfectly blends history, culture, and seaside fun.

  • Location: East Coast
  • Best Time: May – October
  • Known For: Medina, beaches, nightlife
